# Design System

## Philosophy
The UX should be polished and intuitive, removing the complexity of accounting from the user while making the results transparent.

## Key UX Components

### 1. Financial Health Indicator
A clear visual score (out of 100) on the dashboard synthesizing profitability, receivables, and cash position.

### 2. Smart Alerts
Clear, visually distinct notifications (Red/Yellow/Green) highlighting business risks derived from accounting data.

### 3. Payment Risk Badges
Contextual indicators on invoices showing the likelihood of late payment based on past data.

### 4. "Explain This Number" Modals
Interactive elements on reports that break down a single aggregated metric into its constituent transactions.

### 5. "What Changed?" / Accounting Impact View
A visual timeline or summary panel that appears after a transaction, explicitly showing the generated journal entries (Debit/Credit) and the impact on reports (P&L/Balance Sheet).
