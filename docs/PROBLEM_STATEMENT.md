# Problem Statement

We are addressing the "Urban Furniture Accounting System" problem under the brand name **Modura**.
The goal is to build an end-to-end accounting workflow that handles master data, transactions (purchases/sales), invoicing, payments, and double-entry accounting ledgers.

Our key insight is that a standard CRUD app won't win. We must build **"Modura: Smart Accounting & Quiet Luxury Financial OS"**, demonstrating a robust accounting engine overlaid with rule-based financial intelligence, an immersive welcome experience, and high-quality UX that explains the financial impact of every user action.

## Status & Milestones
- **Completed**: Zero-sidebar mega-menu, Quiet Luxury elevated wireframe cards, financial reports normalization, trial balance verification, and Full Journal Ledger navigation with double-entry impact cards.
